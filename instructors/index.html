Already built

Coded, published, in your students' hands.

BreatheToo has been on the App Store since August 2026. Free, no ads, no account. Twelve languages. Its engine plays every kind of breathing: Power Breathing, cardiac coherence, box breathing, 4-7-8, or any rhythm you build yourself, with your own rounds and retention.

Your students close their eyes: animated visuals, haptic guidance, and for Power Breathing a voice coach in their language.
