The rules we play by

Clear from day one.

Your sessions are yours

They carry your name and BreatheToo's vocabulary: Power Breathing. We use no third-party trademark, badge or logo. Your credentials are written by you, in your words.

Independent

BreatheToo is not affiliated with any certification body and carries no third-party brand. If you hold a certification, read your own instructor terms before joining — you stay responsible for them.

Free, for everyone

Your students pay nothing. Founding instructors pay nothing, ever. No ads, no tracking, no data sold — the same promise the app makes to everyone.
